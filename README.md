# Revenue Performance & Growth Analysis for Management Decision-Making Dashboard (Power BI)

## 1. Business Problem
Management needed visibility into revenue performance across products and regions to identify growth drivers, underperforming areas, and opportunities to improve overall sales performance.

## 2. Dataset Overview
- Sales transactions dataset
- Fields: date, product, region, units sold, unit price, revenue
- Time period covered

## 3. Key Business Questions
- Which products and regions contribute most to total revenue?
- How is revenue trending month-over-month?
- Are revenue changes driven more by pricing or sales volume?
- Which products show declining or unstable performance?
- What areas present opportunities for revenue growth?

## 4. Analysis Approach
- Data cleaning and validation
- Revenue and trend analysis
- Product and regional performance analysis
- KPI calculation (growth, contribution, averages)
- Scenario-based analysis for decision support

## 5. Key Insights
- Phone consistently generated the highest revenue across Lagos and Abuja, accounting for the largest share of total sales. Thi performance was driven by higher sales volumes rather than premium pricing.
- Lagos was the top-performing city, contributing the highest total revenue and maintaining relatively stable month-to-month sales compared to other cities.
- Abuja showed moderate sales volume but lower revenue contribution, indicating a less favorable product mix or lower average selling prices.
- Several products recorded sporadic sales across multiple cities, resulting in high revenue volatility and minimal contribution to overall performance.
- Revenue growth periods aligned with spikes in unit sales rather than price changes, confirming that demand volume was the primary driver of revenue changes.

## 6. Management Recommendations
- Maintain strong inventory availability and promotional focus for Phone and Laptop, as they consistently drive the majority of revenue.
- Increase sales focus in Lagos by ensuring adequate stock levels and targeted campaigns to sustain momentum.
- Review pricing, product mix, or discount strategies Ibadan, where sales activity does not translate proportionally into revenue.
- Consider consolidating or discontinuing low-volume products that show high revenue volatility and limited strategic value.
- Implement monthly city-level performance tracking to quickly identify declining regions and adjust inventory or pricing strategies.
- Use historical sales trends to inform quarterly revenue targets and support more accurate forecasting.

## 7. Tools Used
- SQL (data querying and aggregation)
- Excel (data cleaning and validation)
- Power BI (data modeling, DAX, dashboards)
